[sticky] wolff's law

bone adapts to load. more load → denser along stress lines. less → thinner.

mechanism
fluid shear on osteocytes → osteoblasts deposit matrix along force direction.
>safe zone ~650–3000 microstrain >braces: low continuous load for months >punching your face: acute trauma, not remodeling

zhang's extension (shape change post-maturity) is real. bone smashing still isn't.

[sticky] bone smashing — don't

high risk / no cosmetic evidence / platforms ban tutorials for a reason
>claim: impact → wolff → hunter eyes >reality: inflammation, osteoclasts, asymmetry

forums cite real physics then apply it like shin conditioning. facial bones ≠ tibia. attribution on "results" is cooked (puberty, cut, gear).

[sticky] neck protocol

scm frames the jaw. train it.

1. flexion 3×12–20 — off bed edge, chin tucked
2. extension 3×12–15 — prone, full rom
3. side flexion 3×12–15/side — hand resistance, highest roi
4. chin tucks 3×10 — posture, daily

>visible ~8–12 weeks if you're not skipping

[sticky] supps

d3+k2 (mk-7) — directs ca to bone, not random soft tissue

creatine 3–5g — mass/strength, bloat meme is overrated at low bf

high k / low na — less face puff, cheaper than cope diuretics

>skip collagen if protein already fine >skip "jawmaxx blend" amazon slop

[sticky] realistic timeline

>weeks 1–4: posture + less bloat >weeks 4–8: scm starting to show >weeks 8–16: actually noticeable >6–18mo: compounding if you're young

male face still cooks into mid-20s. patience or ngmi thread.
